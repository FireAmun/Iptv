## Recommended direction: refined Cinema

Preserve the approved brand rather than introduce an unrelated theme. Use near-black `#0B0B0B`, charcoal `#181A18`, warm-white `#FFFBF2`, cream `#F2ECDD`, saffron `#F5CB5C`, and accessible light-mode gold `#765615`. English uses Manrope; Arabic uses Cairo. Gold marks primary actions/focus, not every large surface. Cards/sheets use disciplined 12–20dp corners; a 4/8dp spacing rhythm and 48dp minimum phone touch targets unify screens. TV uses larger type/targets and explicit focus rings.

Alternative directions considered: keep current shapes and only recolour (smallest diff but leaves hierarchy/navigation problems), or introduce an entirely new theme (larger brand disruption). Refined Cinema addresses the audit while retaining the approved palette and architecture.

## Functional follow-ups, outside UI-only scope

### Continue Watching and saved playback positions

Current LifecyclePlayerSession creates/release a player with no persistent resume contract. Implement source-scoped stable movie/provider-episode identity, stored position/duration/update time/completion, lifecycle/periodic saves, refresh-safe matching, real restart restoration, Continue/Start over and completed-item handling in a separate approved functional slice. Existing episode row IDs are transient; do not use them as a global resume identity. Planned previews must be marked as future work.

### 300,000 entries and loading-screen wording

Actual current constant is 100,000 entries. Parser/network payload budget is 64 MiB per response; imports still materialize parsed records before atomic save. A constant-only change is insufficient evidence for 300k support. Validate import, memory, cancellation, encrypted save/refresh, FTS migration/search, cached startup/paging and real low-memory TV behavior at 300k. Assess response-byte and deadline budgets from measurements. Only then show truthful copy such as “Supports playlists with up to 300,000 entries”; this means catalogue capacity, not supplied channels. Do not advertise 300k in the current build or add an artificial startup delay.
