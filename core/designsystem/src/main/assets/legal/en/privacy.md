# Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Amar Iptv, a media player app made by Amar Elshaer, handles information. Contact: gamerammar343@gmail.com

## Summary

- Your sources, logins, favourites and watch progress stay on your device.
- There are no accounts, no advertising, and we never sell information.
- Anonymous usage statistics help us improve the app. They are on by default and you can turn them off at any time.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the sources you add (playlist addresses, files, server addresses, usernames and passwords), the catalogue loaded from them, your favourites, Continue Watching and playback positions, and settings such as language and appearance.

Sources and logins are encrypted with a key kept in Android's secure key store. This information is not sent to us. Android backup is turned off for the app, so it is not copied to cloud backups.

## Connections the app makes for you

To load and play content, the app connects directly to the addresses and servers that you add, including to load channel logos and posters. Those providers receive your device's IP address and the details of each request, and their own privacy policies apply.

A source that uses http:// instead of https:// is not encrypted on the network. The app warns you before it uses one.

## Anonymous usage statistics

When usage statistics are on, the app sends a limited set of events to PostHog, a service that processes them for us on servers in the United States:

- the app was opened, moved to the background, installed or updated;
- a source was added or refreshed, with its type (file, address or server login) and a rough size range of its catalogue;
- playback was started, with the type of item (live, film, series or episode);
- a favourite was added or removed, with the type of item;
- the type of error when the app fails, without error details;
- app and device details: app version, Android version, device maker and model, device type, screen size, language, time zone and whether it is an emulator, plus random identifiers for the installation and the session.

These events never include channel or title names, playlist or server addresses, usernames, passwords, or what you watch. Location lookup from your IP address is turned off, and we do not build user profiles.

No statistics are sent before you accept the Terms of Use when you first open the app. You can turn usage statistics off at any time in Settings, under Legal & About.

We keep statistics only as long as they are needed to understand how the app works and to fix problems.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information for advertising. Usage statistics are shared only with PostHog as our service provider, or when the law requires it.

## Your choices

- Remove a source in the app at any time.
- Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored on your device.
- Turn usage statistics off in Settings, under Legal & About.
- Statistics are anonymous, so we usually cannot link them to you. You can still email us with any question or deletion request.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Security

We protect stored sources with encryption, but no method of storage or transfer is completely secure.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we will update the version and date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
